## The finding: the largest addressable term this project has ever surfaced

`aglio olio recipe` — **6,600/mo in Malaysia, LOW competition.** Eighteen near-identical phrasings
return the same figure (`aglio e olio recipe`, `spaghetti aglio olio recipe`, `recipe for aglio
olio`…), and the commercial variant `extra virgin olive oil for aglio olio` runs 170/mo at MEDIUM.

Four rounds of research never found it because every seed was a *product* seed — "olive oil",
"olive oil Malaysia", "olive oil price". This is a **dish** seed, and the dish is olive oil and
garlic. Nothing else in five rounds is within an order of magnitude of it: the previous best was
`extra virgin olive oil salad dressing` at 390.

**The lesson for the flow: seed the dishes a product is an ingredient in, not only the product.**

## What the rest of the market looks like on the fifth pass

**English** still carries everything. Beyond the pasta cluster, the unclaimed families are the
**avocado-oil comparison** (~330 across all phrasings), the **balsamic pairing** (~200), and
genuine long tails around **hummus** (~60), **grilling** (~50) and **cloudy oil** (~10).

**Malay has gone to zero.** This is a change from round 4, not a repeat of it. Every seed in this
round's Malay pull — pasta, ikan bakar, keruh, minyak bijan, sos salad, raya — returned
**0/mo**, and the single term still showing any volume at all is `minyak zaitun vs minyak sawit`
at 10. Round 3 called Malay "exhausted at the head"; round 4 found a 0–10 tail; **round 5 finds no
measurable Malay demand for any remaining angle.** The six Malay articles below are published for
topic-map completeness and AI-answer coverage, and D39 records that plainly rather than dressing it
up as demand.

**Arabic inside Malaysia is unchanged at a 0–10/mo ceiling.** The pull surfaced one large new
family — `أنواع الزيتون` and its dozen variants — but most of that traffic is agricultural
(`أفضل أنواع شتلات الزيتون`, `أفضل أنواع السماد للزيتون`: seedlings and fertiliser, people planting
trees, not buying oil). The varieties article below is written for the buyer, and the growing
queries are discarded.
