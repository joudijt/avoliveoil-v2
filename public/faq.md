# FAQ — Arabian Village

## What is Arabian Village olive oil?

A premium first cold pressed extra virgin olive oil from centuries-old groves in Palestine, hand-harvested and mechanically pressed within hours of picking, with no heat and no chemical processing. 100% Palestinian olive oil — no blending, no additives.

## Is it halal?

The oil is plant-based, alcohol-free and contains no animal-derived ingredients, which is why it suits Halal households.

## Is it vegan?

Yes. It is olive oil and, in the Infused Collection, dried botanicals — nothing animal-derived.

## What is extra virgin, exactly?

The top grade: mechanically extracted with no heat or solvents, and free acidity under 0.8%. This oil tests below 0.028%.

## What sizes does it come in?

250 ml and 500 ml, for the classic oil and for every infused flavour.

## What is in the Infused Collection?

Six flavours, each extra virgin olive oil slow-infused with whole dried botanicals: Lavender & Chamomile, Blackseed (Habbat al-Barakah), Turmeric & Black Pepper, Rosemary & Basil, Fennel & Anise, Wild Thyme (Za'atar).

## Why dried botanicals rather than fresh?

Fresh plant matter carries water, and water in oil is what spoils an infusion. Dried botanicals let the oil keep normally.

## Can I cook with infused olive oil, or is it only for finishing?

Both work, but the aroma is the point and heat drives it off — most of it is better used as a finishing oil, or added late.

## How should I store it?

Cool, dark and closed. Heat, light and air are what age an olive oil; a cupboard away from the hob is better than a shelf beside it.

## Does it have a harvest date?

Yes, every bottle carries one.
