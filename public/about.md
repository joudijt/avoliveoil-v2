# About — Arabian Village

Arabian Village is a premium, first cold pressed extra virgin olive oil sourced from centuries-old olive groves in Palestine, hand-harvested and mechanically pressed within hours of picking without heat or chemicals. The range is the classic Palestinian extra virgin olive oil plus a six-flavour Infused Collection — extra virgin olive oil slow-infused with whole dried botanicals: lavender & chamomile, blackseed (habbat al-barakah), turmeric & black pepper, rosemary & basil, fennel & anise, and wild thyme (za'atar). Sold in Malaysia exclusively through Berkat Madinah Store, in RM59.00 (250 ml) and RM103.00 (500 ml).
